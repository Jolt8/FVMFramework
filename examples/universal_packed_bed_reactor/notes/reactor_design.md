In this file, I will explain how the reactor was built

Main body
    - The main body of the reactor is a 12 inch long, 0.5 inch OD steel pipe with 0.5 inch NPT threads on both ends
    - Two 0.5 inch NPT to 1/4 inch compression fittings are screwed onto the pipe which copper pipes are then attatched to

Layering
    - Starting from the steel pipe wall and moving outwards, there is (in order):
        1. 1 piece of mica tape placed in a doughnut around the reactor to prevent the thermocouple from grounding out
        2. A thin (~3-5 mm) layer of fiberglass tape placed in a doughnut around the reactor to prevent the thermocouple from grounding out
        3. The thermocouple itself with the wire travelling along the length of the reactor downwards
        4. 1 piece of mica tape placed in a doughnut around the reactor to prevent the thermocouple from grounding out
        5. A thin (~3-5 mm) layer of fiberglass tape placed in a doughnut around the reactor to prevent the thermocouple from grounding out
        6. A double layer of more mica tape wrapped around everything to enclose it and insulate it from the heating wire
        7. 2.7 m of 22 guage Kanthal heating wire wrapped tightly around the entire assembly 
        8. A 1 inch thick layer of ceramic blanket wrapped around the reactor secured in place by wire
        9. A layer of aluminum foil wrapped around the ceramic blanket secured in place by wire

Packing Media and Catalysts
    - Before the first packing media was placed down, a circular piece of copper mesh was put at the bottom of the reactor to prevent the silicon carbide sand from dropping down into the pipe below (this has worked well)
    - For the first 3 inches of the reactor, there is silicon carbide sand with a particle size of 60-90 mesh 
        - This is to act as a preheater to allow all the reactants to boil and reach the desired temperature without reacting
        - If this was just more copper mesh, decoupling the heat of vaporization from the heat of reaction would be difficult
        - This material was chosen because it's thermally conductive and it doesn't react with almost anything under most conditions
    - Then there is another circular piece of copper mesh that prevents the silicon carbide from getting up in the copper mesh catalyst
    - For the remainin 9 inche up to the top of the reactor, there is a monolithic piece of copper mesh 
    - For the remaining 9 inches up to the top, there is is a long piece of rolled up 100 mesh copper mesh
    - then there is a final circular piece of copper just because
